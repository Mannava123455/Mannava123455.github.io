About me

I'm a communications and signal processing engineer focused on real-time GNSS receivers. I'm pursuing an M.Tech in Communications & Signal Processing at IIT Hyderabad, where I work as a research assistant in the 5G Testbed R&D Lab.

My work started with a complete GPS L1 C/A baseband receiver in floating-point C: acquisition, tracking, bit and frame synchronisation, and position estimation. I validated it on live signals captured with a BladeRF SDR. I now port and optimise that receiver for a resource-constrained CEVA DSP using fixed-point arithmetic.

I like the part of the job where the maths meets the hardware: working out where precision is lost, what each stage costs in cycles and memory, and what the noise floor of every block really is. Technologies I work with daily:

  • MATLAB

Where I've worked

M.Tech Research Assistant at 5G Testbed R&D Lab, IIT Hyderabad

August 2024 to present

  • Ported and optimised the GPS L1 C/A baseband receiver to a CEVA DSP in fixed-point C, on a platform with a 153 MHz clock and 512 KB of on-chip memory for code and data.
  • Brought tracking down to 160 µs per satellite, which leaves room for real-time multi-channel processing within the compute budget.
  • Ran extensive Monte Carlo tests across geographic locations and signal conditions. Positioning matches the floating-point implementation.
  • Analysed the whole receiver chain mathematically and characterised the noise performance and sensitivity of each processing stage.
  • Reached about -140 dBm acquisition and -150 dBm tracking sensitivity, with a median positioning accuracy near 7 m.
  • Resolved fixed-point precision, complexity, memory and real-time problems that appeared during DSP integration.

Things I've built

GPS L1 C/A receiver on a DSP

My M.Tech thesis. A real-time GPS L1 C/A baseband receiver in fixed-point C on a CEVA DSP, built to be integrated into an NB-IoT chipset for navigation and NTN (non-terrestrial network) applications.

The aim is a receiver small and sensitive enough for commercial GNSS-enabled products, not just a lab demo.
